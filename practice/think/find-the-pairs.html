Find the pairs

Find the pairs is one of the 6 brain games in Bonet, in the group Memory. In the words of the app: Turn over cards and find the matching ones. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

What you do

Cards lie face down. You turn over two at a time. If they are the same, they stay open. If not, they turn back over and you try again.

The game ends when you have found every pair, or when the time for that level runs out.

You play by tapping the screen.

What the difficulty levels change

Easy3 pairs, 6 cards
Medium6 pairs, 12 cards
Hard8 pairs, 16 cards

The time limit grows with the number of cards: 90, 120 and 150 seconds.

The easiest level is in the free plan. The other levels need Premium. The app suggests a level from your own recent games, one step at a time. It is only a suggestion, and you choose.

What the app shows afterwards

After a game the app shows the pairs you found, the time and the number of tries. These numbers describe how one game went. The game is for practice. It is not a test of memory, attention or thinking.
