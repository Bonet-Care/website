Tap in order

Tap in order is one of the 6 brain games in Bonet, in the group Thinking. In the words of the app: Tap numbers from small to big, then numbers and letters. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

What you do

Numbers are scattered over the screen. You tap them in order, from the smallest to the biggest. The app shows which one comes next.

On the hardest level, numbers and letters take turns: a number, then a letter, then the next number.

You play by tapping the screen.

What the difficulty levels change

EasyNumbers 1 to 6
MediumNumbers 1 to 9
Hard10 items, numbers and letters

Each game has a few rounds, with the numbers in new places every round.

The easiest level is in the free plan. The other levels need Premium. The app suggests a level from your own recent games, one step at a time. It is only a suggestion, and you choose.

What the app shows afterwards

After a game the app shows how many you tapped right, the time and the number of wrong taps. These numbers describe how one game went. The game is for practice. It is not a test of memory, attention or thinking.
