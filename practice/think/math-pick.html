Math pick

Math pick is one of the 6 brain games in Bonet, in the group Thinking. In the words of the app: Work out the sum and pick the right answer. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

What you do

A sum is shown with several answers. You work it out and pick the right answer before the time for that question runs out.

You can do A set of questions, one at a time, or choose Keep going and practise for a few minutes.

You can play in two ways: Tap the screen, or Use the camera. With the camera you hold your hand over an answer for a moment and it is chosen. The camera picture stays on your device.

What the difficulty levels change

EasySmall numbers, + and −
MediumBigger numbers, + and −
HardAdds times (×)

Harder levels also show more answers to choose from (4, 5 and 6) and give less time for each question (25, 20 and 15 seconds).

The easiest level is in the free plan. The other levels need Premium. The app suggests a level from your own recent games, one step at a time. It is only a suggestion, and you choose.

What the app shows afterwards

After a game the app shows your points and how many questions you got right. These numbers describe how one game went. The game is for practice. It is not a test of memory, attention or thinking.
