Remember the order

Remember the order is one of the 6 brain games in Bonet, in the group Memory. In the words of the app: Watch the squares light up, then tap them in order. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

What you do

A grid of squares is shown. Some of the squares light up, one after another. When it is your turn, you tap the same squares in the same order.

When you get a list right, the next one is one square longer. After a miss you get a new list of the same length. The game ends after two misses in a row, or when you reach the longest list for that level.

You play by tapping the screen.

What the difficulty levels change

Easy6 squares, starts with 2
Medium9 squares, starts with 2
Hard9 squares, starts with 3

On harder levels the squares also light up faster, and the longest list grows from 6 to 9 squares.

The easiest level is in the free plan. The other levels need Premium. The app suggests a level from your own recent games, one step at a time. It is only a suggestion, and you choose.

What the app shows afterwards

After a game the app shows the longest list you remembered, the seconds per tap and the number of wrong taps. These numbers describe how one game went. The game is for practice. It is not a test of memory, attention or thinking.
