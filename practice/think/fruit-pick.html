Fruit pick

Fruit pick is one of the 6 brain games in Bonet, in the group Attention. In the words of the app: Pick the right fruit before time runs out. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

What you do

Pictures of fruit appear on the screen. Bonet names one, and you pick it before the time for that round runs out.

There are three ways to play: Pick what I ask for, picking every apple on the screen, or Keep going, which runs for a few minutes.

You can play in two ways: Tap the screen, or Use the camera. With the camera you hold your hand over an answer for a moment and it is chosen. The camera picture stays on your device.

What the difficulty levels change

When Bonet names one item, or when you keep going for a few minutes, there are two levels:

EasyOnly a few on screen
HardMore on screen

When you pick every one of a kind, there are three levels:

Level 13 things, 1 to find
Level 25 things, 2 to find
Level 37 things, 2 to find

The easiest level is in the free plan. The other levels need Premium. The app suggests a level from your own recent games, one step at a time. It is only a suggestion, and you choose.

What the app shows afterwards

After a game the app shows your points and how many rounds you got right. These numbers describe how one game went. The game is for practice. It is not a test of memory, attention or thinking.
