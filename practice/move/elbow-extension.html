Elbow Extension

Elbow Extension is one of the 34 movement exercises in Bonet. A 3D guide shows the move, and your camera counts each repetition on your device. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

At a glance

  • Body area: Arm and shoulder
  • Position: can be done seated
  • Sides: one side at a time
  • Level in the app: 2 of 3
  • Tracking: the camera follows your body
  • Plan: Premium. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises.

What you do

On the exercise screen, the app gives this instruction:

Straighten your arm from a bent position.

While you move, short cues appear on the screen and are spoken aloud, in this order:

  1. Straighten arm
  2. Hold straight
  3. Bend arm

You can switch the voice off in Settings. You can skip, shorten or repeat any exercise.

What the camera counts

The camera follows the angle at the elbow, from shoulder to elbow to wrist. It reads whichever arm is straighter.

A repetition is counted when you reach the position, hold it for about one second, and then come back to the start. The next repetition begins after you have returned.

When your hips are in the picture, the app also checks that your body stays upright. If you lean, it reminds you to keep your body straight and does not count until you do.

  • The count is an estimate. Light, camera angle and loose clothing can change it.
  • Camera video is read on your device. It is never recorded or uploaded.
  • If the camera does not suit you, choose Do it without the camera in the app and tap a button to count each repetition yourself.

How to set up your phone

Put your phone, tablet or computer where the camera can see you. For this exercise the camera needs to see your upper body, with both shoulders in the picture.

This exercise can be done sitting in a stable chair. Use a clear space with good light. If the app cannot see you well enough, it stops counting until you are back in the picture.

Safety

Stop if it hurts or you feel dizzy. Ask your doctor or therapist which exercises are right for you.

Do not rely on the camera to keep you safe. It can miss a loss of balance. What to know before you start
