Sit-to-Stand

Sit-to-Stand is one of the 34 movement exercises in Bonet. A 3D guide shows the move, and your camera counts each repetition on your device. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

At a glance

  • Body area: Legs and walking
  • Position: needs standing
  • Level in the app: 2 of 3
  • Tracking: the camera follows your body
  • Plan: included in the free plan

What you do

On the exercise screen, the app gives this instruction:

Stand up from sitting, then sit back down. Use a chair behind you.

While you move, short cues appear on the screen and are spoken aloud, in this order:

  1. Stand up
  2. Stand tall
  3. Sit down

You can switch the voice off in Settings. You can skip, shorten or repeat any exercise.

What the camera counts

The camera compares the height of your hips with your knees. When your hips rise clearly above your knees, it reads that as standing. When they come back down, it reads that as sitting.

A repetition is counted when you reach the position, hold it for about one second, and then come back to the start. The next repetition begins after you have returned.

  • The count is an estimate. Light, camera angle and loose clothing can change it.
  • Camera video is read on your device. It is never recorded or uploaded.
  • If the camera does not suit you, choose Do it without the camera in the app and tap a button to count each repetition yourself.

How to set up your phone

Put your phone, tablet or computer where the camera can see you. For this exercise the camera needs to see your hips, knees and feet.

This exercise needs you to stand. Stand next to a wall or a sturdy chair you can hold, and have someone nearby if balance is a concern. Use a clear space with good light. If the app cannot see you well enough, it stops counting until you are back in the picture.

Safety

Stop if it hurts or you feel dizzy. Ask your doctor or therapist which exercises are right for you.

Do not rely on the camera to keep you safe. It can miss a loss of balance. What to know before you start
