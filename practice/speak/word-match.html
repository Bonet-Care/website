Word match

Word match is one of the 6 kinds of speaking practice in Bonet. In the words of the app: Remember the cards and find the pairs. It has 100 levels. Bonet is a practice tool, not speech therapy.

Start free

What you do

Cards are shown for a few seconds so you can memorise them: each picture has a matching card with its word. Then the cards turn over.

You turn over two cards at a time to find the pairs. A wrong pair turns back over and you try again. You do not need to speak in this practice.

How hints work

This practice has no hint steps. The cards are shown for a few seconds before each set so you can memorise them, and a wrong pair only turns back over. Nothing is taken away for a miss.

Levels 1 to 100

Each type has 100 levels, so you can begin where it feels comfortable and move up slowly. Items you found hard come back later for review.

The levels are grouped in units of 5, and each level has its own everyday topic.

Levels 1 to 3 are in the free plan. Premium opens all 100 levels.

What is measured

Nothing is taken from the microphone in this practice. The app records that you finished the set and how many wrong pairs you turned over.

Languages

Eight languages: English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German and French. Chinese comes as Cantonese, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese. You practise in the language you choose for the app.

Bonet is practice you do yourself. It does not assess your speech and does not replace a speech-language pathologist. Ask your therapist which practice suits you.
