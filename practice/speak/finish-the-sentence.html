Finish the sentence

Finish the sentence is one of the 6 kinds of speaking practice in Bonet. In the words of the app: Pick the word that fits the sentence. It has 100 levels. Bonet is a practice tool, not speech therapy.

Start free

What you do

A sentence with one word missing is shown, with several words to choose from. You pick the word that fits.

When the sentence is complete, you say the whole sentence aloud.

How hints work

You get a hint. Hints grow one step at a time, so you get just enough help to keep going. Using hints is part of practice, not a mistake.

You can ask for a hint, and after a miss the app offers the next one by itself. The steps in this practice, in order:

  1. The sentence is played again, slowly.
  2. Starts with the sound shown.
  3. Two words are left. The right word and one other stay.
  4. The answer is marked.

Levels 1 to 100

Each type has 100 levels, so you can begin where it feels comfortable and move up slowly. Items you found hard come back later for review.

The levels are grouped in units of 5, and each level has its own everyday topic. You pass a level with 60 percent or more, and passing opens the next one. The percentage counts the items you did with no hint, or with only a repeat of the prompt.

Levels 1 to 3 are in the free plan. Premium opens all 100 levels.

What is measured

After you speak, the app shows a few plain numbers about that try:

  • how many of the words or characters it heard;
  • your pace, as words, characters or sounds a minute;
  • how long you took to start;
  • how many short pauses there were.

These are practice indicators, not a test or an assessment. Recognition can miss words you did say, so a low number may be the app's mistake and not yours.

Recognition runs on your device where the device supports it. Otherwise it is handled by Apple (on iPhone and iPad) or Google (on the web, for several languages). Bonet keeps the recognised text and the measures. It does not keep voice recordings. See what Bonet tracks.

Languages

Eight languages: English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German and French. Chinese comes as Cantonese, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese. You practise in the language you choose for the app.

Bonet is practice you do yourself. It does not assess your speech and does not replace a speech-language pathologist. Ask your therapist which practice suits you.
