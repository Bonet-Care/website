Legs and walking: 4 exercises to practise at home

These exercises move the legs: marching on the spot, standing up from a chair and sitting down again, straightening one knee while seated, and rising onto the toes.

A 3D guide shows each move, and your camera counts the repetitions on your device. 1 of these 4 exercises are in the free plan.

Start free

The exercises

  • Gait Pre-training: March in place, lifting knees high. (needs standing)
  • Sit-to-Stand: Stand up from sitting, then sit back down. Use a chair behind you. (needs standing, free plan)
  • Seated Knee Extension: While seated, straighten one leg out front, then lower it. Alternate legs. (can be done seated)
  • Standing Heel Raises: Standing near a wall or chair, rise onto your toes, then lower. Hold support if needed. (needs standing)

How to set up your phone

Put your phone, tablet or computer where the camera can see you. Each exercise page says what the camera needs to see. Use a clear space with good light. If the app cannot see you well enough, it stops counting until you are back in the picture.

  • The count is an estimate. Light, camera angle and loose clothing can change it.
  • Camera video is read on your device. It is never recorded or uploaded.

Safety

Stop if it hurts or you feel dizzy. Ask your doctor or therapist which exercises are right for you.

Do not rely on the camera to keep you safe. It can miss a loss of balance. What to know before you start
