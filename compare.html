What others do better

  • Constant Therapy reports a company-run Phase II trial of its app in post-stroke aphasia, holds an FDA Breakthrough Device designation (priority review, not authorisation) and has a separate Clinician App.
  • Tactus Therapy has 19 single-purpose apps made by a certified speech-language pathologist, with a cueing hierarchy, bought once.
  • TalkPath is free and lists more than 13,500 exercises, far more than Bonet has.
  • Flint Rehab sells an FDA-listed device with four published studies, and a remote programme where a licensed therapist builds the plan.

Bonet has not been tested in a clinical trial and is not a medical device. How long each company has been in the market was not stated on the pages we read, so we make no claim about it.

Sources for the cost board

The cost board starts with no visit cost filled in. What a person pays per therapy visit depends on their plan, deductible, copay or coinsurance and visit limits, and we found no per-visit figure we could stand behind. Enter your own. The other outputs do not need it.

  • Chevan J, Riddle DL, Reed M. Out-of-pocket spending for ambulatory physical therapy services from 2008 to 2012: national panel survey. Physical Therapy, 2015. Uses the Medical Expenditure Panel Survey (2,189 people). It reports spending per episode of care, not per visit: among people with any out-of-pocket spending the mean was $351 and the median $144, over an average of 9.9 visits. Abstract.
  • Skydel J, Ross J. Commercial negotiated rates for outpatient physical therapy at US hospitals. JAMA Internal Medicine, research letter, 2026. Median negotiated rates for evaluation services ran from $151 to $215 across hospitals. These are what insurers pay, not what patients pay. Yale News summary.

We found no comparable source for speech-language therapy. A month is counted as 52 ÷ 12 weeks. Premium price and trial length come from the same file that sets the price on the home page.
