For family and carers: helping someone practise at home

A family member or carer can set Bonet up for someone else, with that person's agreement. Bonet then suggests a short daily plan of movement, speaking and brain games, and keeps a one-page report the person can print or share. Nothing is sent to you automatically. It is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

How do I set Bonet up for someone else?

  1. Make sure the person agrees to use Bonet and has checked with their doctor or therapist.
  2. Open app.bonet.care on their phone, tablet or computer. When the app asks who will use it, say you are setting it up for someone else.
  3. Answer the setup questions together. Turn on Simple mode and large text if a calmer screen helps.
  4. Clear a safe space with good light and a stable chair, and stay near for balance exercises.

What is in the one-page report?

  • The days practised, the minutes and the current streak.
  • The exercises done, with sessions and repetitions.
  • Speaking: tries, words recognised, pace and pauses.
  • Brain games played and items right.
  • A note the person can add, and questions to bring to the therapist.

The free plan covers the last two weeks; Premium covers longer ranges. The report shows what was practised. It is not a medical record or a clinical assessment. See an example report.

What is shared, and with whom?

  • The report is shared only when the person prints it or shares it. Bonet does not send it to family or therapists.
  • Camera video is analysed on the device and never recorded or uploaded. Voice recordings are not kept.
  • Other players in the weekly league see only a nickname and points.
  • Bonet does not sell data or show ads.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

What should I watch for as a carer?

You know the person best. Stop the session if they seem unwell, in pain or dizzy, and call your local emergency number for sudden face drooping, weakness or trouble speaking. Bonet does not watch over anyone and cannot call for help.

Can I see their results from my own phone?

Not today. There are no dashboards, remote monitoring or alerts in the app. The report is the way to look at practice together, and it can start a conversation with the therapist.

What does it cost?

You can start free. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises, speaking levels 1 to 3 and brain games on Easy. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free, and you can cancel any time.
