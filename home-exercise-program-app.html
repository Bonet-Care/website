A home exercise program app: keep up your practice at home

If a physical therapist gave you a home exercise program, you may want help doing it regularly. Bonet is a daily home practice app with a short daily plan, camera counting of repetitions and a record of the days you practised. It does not import or replace your therapist's program, and it is not a medical device. Check any exercise with your therapist before you do it.

Does Bonet import or replace my therapist's home exercise program?

No. Bonet cannot import your program, and a therapist cannot prescribe or assign exercises in it. Your therapist's program stays the plan to follow.

Bonet has its own library of 34 movement exercises, for shoulders and arms, hands, sit-to-stand, legs and balance. You choose which ones to practise. If one looks like an exercise in your program, ask your therapist whether it suits you and how often to do it.

Ask your therapist which exercises suit you; stop if anything hurts.

How can Bonet help me practise regularly?

  • A short daily plan, so you do not have to decide what to do each day. Each step is ticked when it is done, and you can skip, shorten or repeat any exercise.
  • Coming with the iPhone and iPad app: a daily practice reminder at a time you choose, off until you turn it on.
  • A record of the days and minutes you practised, and a streak, so you can see how regular you have been.

How does the camera count my exercises?

Put your phone, tablet or computer where the camera can see you. A model on your device finds body and hand positions in the picture, and simple rules count each repetition and time the set. After a set you get a short tip, for example about height, steadiness, or left and right.

  • Camera video is read on your device. It is never recorded or uploaded.
  • The count is an estimate. Light, camera angle and loose clothing can change it.
  • Do not rely on the camera to keep you safe. It can miss a loss of balance.

Can I show my therapist what I practised?

Yes, if you choose. Bonet makes a one-page report of the days, minutes and exercises you practised. You print it or share it yourself; Bonet does not send it to anyone. It is a summary of home practice, not a medical record or a clinical assessment, and it does not show whether you did your therapist's program. See an example report.

What should I check with my therapist?

  • Which exercises suit you, how many to do and how often.
  • Before you start, and whenever the app and your therapist say different things. Do what your therapist says.
  • If an exercise hurts, feels too hard, or you are not sure it is safe for you.

What does it cost?

You can start free. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises, speaking levels 1 to 3 and brain games on Easy. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free, and you can cancel any time.
