Frequently asked questions

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Getting started

What is Bonet?

Bonet is an app for daily practice at home. It has exercises counted with your camera, speaking practice, brain games, a weekly league and a progress page. It is made with older adults in mind.

Do I need a doctor's approval?

Bonet does not require it, but we ask you to check with your doctor or therapist before you start, especially if you have had a stroke or have balance problems. They know what is safe for you.

What do I need?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone, and a space to move. The camera is used for exercises and the microphone for speaking practice. You can use the games without either.

Safety and health

Is Bonet a medical device? Does it treat or diagnose?

No. Bonet is a practice tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it has not been reviewed or cleared by the FDA. The numbers it shows are estimates for practice.

What if I feel pain or dizzy?

Stop right away and rest. If it does not pass, or you have signs of a stroke such as face drooping, weakness or trouble speaking, call 911 or your local emergency number. Bonet cannot call for help.

Can Bonet tell me if I am getting better?

It shows what you did and how your numbers change over time. It cannot tell you about your health, because the numbers can be affected by light, camera angle and how well the app hears your voice. Ask your clinician how to read your progress.

Privacy

Does Bonet record my video or my voice?

No. Camera video is analyzed on your device and is not recorded or uploaded. We do not save voice recordings. We save the results, such as repetition counts, the words the app recognized, pace and pauses.

Who sees my data?

You, and us as the operator, with a small number of service providers that run Bonet for us. Other players in your league see only your nickname and points. Anyone else sees your information only if you share a report with them. Read the Privacy Policy.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use advertising trackers.

Is Bonet covered by HIPAA?

HIPAA is the US law for health care providers and health plans. A consumer app like Bonet is generally not covered by it. Other privacy laws apply and we follow them. We do not claim to be "HIPAA compliant".

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and choose Delete account. This erases your account and your practice history right away. If you subscribed through Apple or Google, you also need to cancel the subscription in your phone's subscription settings.

Speech and camera

Why does Bonet miss words I said?

Speech recognition can be less accurate with some accents, quiet or slow speech, background noise, and speech changed by a stroke. If it keeps missing words, move closer to the microphone, find a quiet place, or use the hints. A low score may be the app's mistake, not yours.

The camera does not count my repetitions right.

Try more light, put the device at about chest height a few steps away, and make sure your whole upper body or the joints being tracked are visible. Loose clothing and a busy background can confuse it. If it still miscounts, keep practicing and ignore the count that day. You can report it at infos@coglixlabs.com.

Plans and billing

What does Premium cost and what is the free trial?

Premium costs US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free for new subscribers. The price and the date of the first charge are shown before you pay. A free plan is always available.

How do I cancel?

On the web, use the Billing page in the app, or email us. On iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Bonet. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your account cancels a web subscription but not an Apple one.

Can I get a refund?

For App Store purchases, ask Apple. For web payments, see Subscriptions and refunds, and write to us if you were charged in error.

Families and therapists

Can my therapist or family see my results?

Only if you give them. You can print the one-page report or share it. We do not send it to anyone for you.

Can a carer set Bonet up for me?

Yes, with your agreement. Bonet is for adults, and a family member or carer can help. Please check with a clinician and make sure someone is near for balance exercises.

Are the other players in my league real?

Some are, and some are computer players. The league is for fun. You can leave it any time, and that removes your nickname and league results.

Devices

Which devices does Bonet work on?

The web at app.bonet.care in a modern browser. iPhone, iPad and Android apps are not available yet. Your account, plan and progress follow you across devices when you are online.

Which languages is Bonet in?

Eight: English, Cantonese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German and French. The app, the speaking practice and this site are in all eight. Some speech recognition needs an internet connection.

Does it work offline?

Some practice can be done offline. If you finish a session without a connection, it waits on your device for up to 30 days and uploads when you are back online. Speech recognition for some languages needs an internet connection.
