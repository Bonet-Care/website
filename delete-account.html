Delete your Bonet account

You can delete your Bonet account at any time. Bonet is made by Coglix Labs Inc. Deleting removes your account and your practice history straight away. It cannot be undone, and we cannot get anything back.

How do I delete my account in the app?

  1. Open Bonet and sign in.
  2. Go to Me, then Settings.
  3. Choose Delete my account.
  4. To confirm, type your account email or the word shown, then choose Yes, delete everything for good.

If you want a copy first, choose Download my data in Settings before you delete.

Can I ask by email instead?

Yes, for example if you cannot open the app. Email infos@coglixlabs.com from the email address of your Bonet account and write "Delete my account". We will delete it and reply to you.

What is deleted?

  • Your account, your name on the report and your settings.
  • Your practice history: sessions, scores, speaking and movement measures, streaks, points and league results.
  • Your saved plans, goals and reports.
  • The copy of your practice data on the device you delete from. Other devices signed in to the same account lose access straight away and clear their copy when they sign out.

What is kept?

  • Payment records stay with Stripe, Apple or Google under their own rules.
  • Our hosting provider's backups of the database roll off on their own schedule. If a backup is ever restored, we delete your account again.
  • Usage statistics, if you turned them on, are tied to a random number, not to your name or email. You can ask us to delete them too.
  • One anonymous record that an account was deleted, with counts only and no name, email or account number.

What happens to my subscription?

  • A web subscription (paid by card) is cancelled when your account is deleted.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it yourself in the store first.

See the subscriptions page and the privacy policy for more.
