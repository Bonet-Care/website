Movement, speaking and brain games in one app

Bonet combines three kinds of home practice in one daily plan: 34 movement exercises counted by the device camera, speaking practice with hints at 100 levels per practice type, and six short brain games. It is for older adults, including people practising after a stroke. It is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

What is in each part?

  • Move: 34 exercises with a 3D guide. The camera on your device counts each repetition.
  • Speak: say words, name pictures, build sentences and listen, with hints that grow one step at a time. 100 levels per practice type.
  • Think: six short brain games for memory, attention and quick thinking.

How does the daily plan work?

Bonet suggests a short daily plan that mixes movement, speaking and brain games. Each step is ticked when it is done. You can skip, shorten or repeat any exercise.

Do I need special equipment?

No. Bonet runs in a web browser on a phone, tablet or computer with a camera and a microphone. For the movement exercises you need a stable chair and a clear space with good light.

Who is it for?

  • Older adults who want to keep active at home.
  • People practising at home after a stroke or an injury.
  • People practising between physiotherapy or speech therapy visits.
  • Family members and carers who help them.

Which languages is Bonet in?

Eight: English, Cantonese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German and French. The app, the speaking practice and this site are in all eight. Some speech recognition needs an internet connection.

What is Bonet not?

  • It is not a medical device and not a therapy. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
  • It does not replace a doctor, therapist or speech-language pathologist.
  • It has not been tested in a clinical trial, and no regulator has reviewed or cleared it.
  • Its numbers are estimates from a camera and a microphone. They can be wrong.
  • It is a different app from Bold and is not connected to it.

What does it cost?

You can start free. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises, speaking levels 1 to 3 and brain games on Easy. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free, and you can cancel any time.
