What is Bonet?

Bonet is a smaller home practice app with fewer exercises: 34 movement exercises counted by your camera, speaking practice with 100 levels per practice type, and six brain games, in one daily plan. It is free to start. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free.
