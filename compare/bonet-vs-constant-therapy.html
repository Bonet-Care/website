Bonet vs Constant Therapy

Bonet is a daily home practice app for movement, speaking and brain games. This page sets it beside Constant Therapy, using only what Constant Therapy's own public pages said as of November 2026. Where a page did not say, we wrote "not offered, as far as we can tell" or "not checked" instead of guessing. We are not connected to Constant Therapy.

What does Constant Therapy do better than Bonet?

Constant Therapy reports a company-run Phase II trial of its app in post-stroke aphasia, holds an FDA Breakthrough Device designation (priority review, not authorisation) and has a separate Clinician App.

Source: constanttherapyhealth.com/science, constanttherapyhealth.com/science/technology

What is Bonet?

Bonet is a smaller home practice app with fewer exercises: 34 movement exercises counted by your camera, speaking practice with 100 levels per practice type, and six brain games, in one daily plan. It is free to start. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free.
