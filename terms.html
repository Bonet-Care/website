2. What Bonet is

Bonet is an app for daily practice at home. It includes:

  • exercises that use your camera to count repetitions and guide movement, with a 3D guide;
  • speaking practice with hints and automatic measures such as words recognized, pace and pauses;
  • brain games for memory and attention;
  • streaks, points and a weekly league;
  • a progress page and a one-page report you can print or share;
  • a free plan and a paid Premium plan.

Features, limits and prices can change. What each plan includes is shown in the app before you pay.

4. Safety and your responsibility for exercise

Exercise carries risk, including falls, strains and other injury. By using the exercises you accept that risk, to the extent the law lets you.

  • Practice in a clear space with good light and a stable chair, wall or support within reach.
  • If you have balance concerns, practice seated or holding something stable, and have someone nearby.
  • Stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, chest tightness, shortness of breath or feel unwell. Get medical advice before you go on.
  • Do not rely on the camera to protect you from a fall or a wrong movement. The app can miss what is happening.
  • Use your own judgment. You can skip, shorten or stop any exercise.
  • If you help someone else use Bonet, you are responsible for their safety while they practice.
