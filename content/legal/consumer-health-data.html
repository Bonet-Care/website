Bonet Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy applies to "consumer health data" as defined by laws such as the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law. It adds to our Privacy Policy and is meant to be read with it. It applies to people in Washington, Nevada and any other place where a consumer health data law applies, and we give the same choices to everyone in the United States.

Who we are. Coglix Labs Inc., a Delaware corporation. We decide why and how consumer health data is collected and used.

Categories of consumer health data we collect and why

Category Purpose
Health-related answers you give at setup (age band if you choose, weaker side, movement ability, goals, focus areas) To suggest a daily plan that suits you
Exercise results (repetitions, duration, range-of-motion estimates, tracking quality) To count and show your practice, show progress, build your report
Speaking practice results (recognized text, pace, pauses, scores, items and tries) To give feedback, show a trend and choose what to repeat
Brain game results To show progress and choose what to repeat
The fact that you use a practice program for movement, speaking and thinking, to the extent our service, settings or report reveal it To give you the service
Reports you create To let you review and share your practice

We do not collect body images, video or voice recordings (see "What we never collect or store" in the Privacy Policy). We do not collect biometric identifiers, genetic data, precise location, medication or diagnosis records.

Sources. You (what you enter and what you do in the app) and your device (the measures made from your practice). We do not buy health data from anyone and do not collect it from other apps or from your health records.

Consent. We collect and use this data to provide the service you asked for. You choose which setup questions to answer and which features to use. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account in Settings or by emailing us, and we stop collecting for that purpose.

Who we share consumer health data with

Category of recipient Who What Why
Service providers (processors) Supabase (database, sign-in); Cloudflare (hosting); Apple (speech recognition where the device cannot do it, and in-app purchases); Google (speech recognition on the web for some languages) Practice results and speaking measures (Supabase); audio while you speak (speech providers; not stored by us) To run Bonet for you
Other players in your league Other Bonet users who join the same league Your nickname and weekly points only To run the league
People you choose Your therapist, family member or anyone else The report you choose to give them Because you decided
Analytics provider PostHog A random account number and usage events. We do not send practice results, speech text or health answers To understand product use

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it for advertising. We do not sell it to or share it with data brokers. We do not share it with affiliates.

Your rights. Where the law applies, you can:

  1. Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access it, including a list of the third parties and affiliates we have shared it with (the list is above, and we will give the current list on request).
  2. Withdraw consent to our collection or sharing.
  3. Ask us to delete it. We delete it from our systems and tell our processors to do the same. Backups are cleared on the schedule in "How long we keep data" in the Privacy Policy.

How to use your rights. Delete your account in Settings, or email infos@coglixlabs.com from the address on your account. We answer within 45 days and may extend by another 45 days when reasonably needed, and we will tell you if we do.

Appeals. If we deny a request, you can appeal. Reply to our decision with "Privacy Appeal" and a short description of why you disagree. We will answer in writing within 45 days, and tell you what we did and why. If you are not satisfied you can contact your state attorney general: Washington, atg.wa.gov; Nevada, ag.nv.gov.

Contact. Coglix Labs Inc., infos@coglixlabs.com.
