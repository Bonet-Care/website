Bonet Subscriptions, Free Trial, Cancellation and Refunds

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

  • Bonet has a free plan and a paid plan called Premium. The free plan stays free.
  • Premium costs US$9.99 a month. For now, payment on the web is taken in Hong Kong dollars: HK$78 a month, about US$9.99. See section 1.
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. You pay nothing during the trial. If you do not cancel before it ends, your payment method is charged the first monthly price on day 7, and then every month.
  • You can cancel at any time. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds: if you were charged in error, write to us and we will look into it. Your legal rights stay as they are.
  • Where you cancel depends on where you subscribed: on the web, or in the iPhone or iPad app. Section 5 says how.

3. The 7-day free trial

  • Who can have it. New subscribers, once per account. On the web, the trial is given only when the account has never had a subscription. The App Store applies its own rule.
  • What happens. On the day you start, Premium opens at once. You enter a payment card. You are not charged on day 0.
  • When the first charge happens. On day 7, exactly seven days after you started, unless you cancelled before that. The paywall shows the dates before you start.
  • What is charged. The first monthly price, US$9.99 a month (taken as HK$78 on the web for now), or the price the App Store shows.
  • If you cancel during the trial, you pay nothing and keep Premium until the trial ends.
  • If you do nothing, the subscription starts and renews each month until you cancel.
  • Reminders. The date of the first charge is shown before you start, so you can set your own reminder. Do not rely on us to send one.

4. Renewal

  • Premium renews every month on the same day of the month as the first charge (for the web, the day the trial ends) until you cancel.
  • The renewal price is the price in force then. We tell you before a price change (section 10).
  • Apple renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. On the web, cancel before the renewal date.
  • You can see your subscription on the Plan and payment page (web) or in your Apple subscription page.

5. How to cancel

You can cancel at any time.

Web (you subscribed on bonet.care or app.bonet.care)

  1. Sign in to Bonet.
  2. Open Me, then Plan and payment.
  3. Choose Manage / cancel. This opens our payment provider's page (Stripe) for your subscription.
  4. Cancel the subscription and confirm.

You can also email hello@coglixlabs.com from the address on your account and ask us to cancel. Deleting your Bonet account also cancels a web subscription (section 9).

iPhone or iPad (you subscribed through Apple)

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Bonet Premium, then Cancel Subscription (or Cancel Free Trial).
  4. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Deleting your Bonet account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your Apple subscriptions.

6. What happens after you cancel

  • You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for (or until the trial ends, if you cancel during the trial).
  • You are not charged again.
  • After that, your account goes back to the free plan. It does not close, and the results we saved stay in your account. The free plan shows less of your progress than Premium does, and features that are only in Premium lock.
  • You can subscribe again at any time. A free trial is not offered again.

7. Refunds

This section applies to web payments to Coglix Labs Inc. For App Store purchases, ask Apple, for example through Apple's "Report a Problem" page. We do not control Apple's decisions.

  • If you were charged in error (for example twice, or the wrong amount, or a renewal you did not intend), email hello@coglixlabs.com from the address on your account, with the date of the charge. We will look into it and tell you what we decide.
  • Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period. If you cancel, you keep Premium until the period ends.
  • Free trial. There is nothing to refund if you cancel before the trial ends.
  • Your legal rights are not affected. In the EU and UK this includes the right to withdraw within 14 days (section 11). If the law gives you more than this section, you get the law.

Refunds go to the card you paid with, in the currency charged. If your bank changed currency, the amount can differ slightly because of exchange rates.

8. Failed payments

  • If a payment fails (for example an expired card or not enough funds), the subscription may end and your account goes back to the free plan.
  • Apple has its own billing retry rules. It tells you in the store.
  • You can update your card by opening Manage / cancel on the Plan and payment page (web) or in the store.

9. Account deletion is not cancellation

  • Web: deleting your Bonet account in Settings cancels your Stripe subscription first. If we cannot reach Stripe, the deletion stops and nothing is deleted, so you are never left paying for an account that is gone.
  • iPhone and iPad: deleting your Bonet account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel in the store.
  • Cancelling does not delete your account. Your history stays on the free plan until you delete the account.
  • After deletion, payment records stay with Stripe or Apple for tax and fraud rules, as the Privacy Policy explains.

10. Price changes

If we raise the price, we tell you before the new price applies to you, by email or in the app, with the new price and the date. If you do not agree, cancel before that date and you are not charged the new price. Apple may ask for your approval of a price increase in its own flow; the store's rules apply to store subscriptions.

11. Right of withdrawal: European Union and United Kingdom

If you live in the EU or UK, you have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days, without giving a reason, under consumer law (in the UK, the right to cancel).

  • How long. 14 days from the day the contract is made (the day you start the trial or subscribe).
  • How to withdraw. Send us a clear statement, for example by email to hello@coglixlabs.com, or use the model form below. You do not have to use the form.
  • What we refund. We reimburse what the law requires, within 14 days of your withdrawal, using the same payment method. The first 7 days of a new subscription are free, so you pay nothing for the days you used during the trial.
  • We do not ask you to give up this right. You can start using Premium straight away.
  • Apple purchases. Withdraw through the store.
