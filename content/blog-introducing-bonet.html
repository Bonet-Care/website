Why We Built Bonet

The Coglix Labs team, November 2026

A printed sheet of exercises is easy to put aside. It cannot count your reps, show what you did last week, or say well done. We built Bonet to make daily practice at home easier to start and easier to keep track of.

How it works

You need a phone, iPad or computer with a camera. You open the app, choose what to practise, and the app guides you. Three kinds of practice sit in one place.

Movement. The camera finds your body and hand positions on your device, counts reps, estimates range of motion and gives form tips. The video is not recorded or uploaded.

Speaking. You say a word or phrase and the app scores how close it was, in English or Cantonese. We keep the recognised text and the score, not the audio.

Brain games. Short games for memory, attention and quick thinking, with levels of difficulty.

What we do not claim

Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. We have not run a clinical trial and no medical regulator has reviewed it. The measurements are estimates for practice. Ask your therapist or doctor before you start.

We are not trying to replace therapists. The app can produce a practice report that you can print or share, so a therapist or family member can see what you did. What to do about it stays with you and your therapist or doctor.
