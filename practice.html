Practice library

Everything you can practise in Bonet, listed from the app itself: 34 movement exercises, 6 kinds of speaking practice with 100 levels each, and 6 brain games. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

  • Think: 6 brain games

Move

A 3D guide shows each move, and your camera counts the repetitions on your device. Choose by body area, or go straight to the exercises you can do seated.

Arm and shoulder

  • Assisted Shoulder Flexion: Clasp hands together and lift both arms forward. (can be done seated)
  • Shoulder Flexion: Lift one arm forward and upward. (can be done seated, free plan)
  • Bilateral Shoulder Abduction: Lift both arms out to the sides like a T-pose. (can be done seated)
  • Shoulder External Rotation: Elbows pinned to sides at 90°, rotate hands outward. (can be done seated)
  • Bilateral Shoulder Flexion: Lift both arms forward simultaneously (Zombie pose). (can be done seated, free plan)
  • Shoulder Abduction: Lift one arm out to the side. (can be done seated)
  • Assisted Elbow Extension: Clasp hands and push forward to straighten elbows. (can be done seated)
  • Elbow Extension: Straighten your arm from a bent position. (can be done seated)

Hand and fingers

  • Thumb to Index Pinch: Touch thumb tip to index finger tip, then release. (can be done seated)
  • Hand Grasp (Fist): Make a tight fist, then open fully. (can be done seated, free plan)
  • Sequential Tapping: Touch thumb to each finger in order: Index, Middle, Ring, Pinky. Lift thumb between each touch. (can be done seated)
  • Thumb Opposition: Touch thumb tip to the base of pinky finger. (can be done seated)
  • Wrist Extension/Flexion: Wave hand up and down at the wrist. (can be done seated)
  • Finger Spread: Spread fingers wide, then bring together. (can be done seated, free plan)
  • Wrist Radial/Ulnar Deviation: Move hand side to side at the wrist. (can be done seated)
  • Individual Finger Lifts: Place hand flat, lift one finger at a time while others stay down. (can be done seated)
  • Finger Curls: Curl fingers inward toward palm, then extend. (can be done seated)
  • Pinky to Thumb Touch: Touch thumb tip to pinky tip, then release. (can be done seated)
  • Hook Fist: Bend fingers at middle joints while keeping knuckles straight (hook shape). (can be done seated)

Legs and walking

  • Gait Pre-training: March in place, lifting knees high. (needs standing)
  • Sit-to-Stand: Stand up from sitting, then sit back down. Use a chair behind you. (needs standing, free plan)
  • Seated Knee Extension: While seated, straighten one leg out front, then lower it. Alternate legs. (can be done seated)
  • Standing Heel Raises: Standing near a wall or chair, rise onto your toes, then lower. Hold support if needed. (needs standing)

Balance

  • Single Leg Stand: Stand on one leg while keeping your balance. (needs standing)
  • Sit-to-Stand Weight Shift: While seated, lean left and right. (can be done seated, free plan)
  • Standing Weight Shift: Standing, shift hip over left/right foot. (needs standing, free plan)
  • Standing Heel Raises: Standing near a wall or chair, rise onto your toes, then lower. Hold support if needed. (needs standing)

Neck and back

  • Trunk Flexion: While seated, lean forward and return upright. (can be done seated)
  • Neck Side Bend: Slowly tilt your head toward one shoulder, then the other. Keep shoulders still. (can be done seated, free plan)
  • Neck Rotation: Turn your head to look over one shoulder, then the other. Move slowly. (can be done seated)
  • Chin Tuck: Pull your chin straight back (make a double chin). Hold, then release. (can be done seated)
  • Seated Trunk Rotation: While seated, twist your upper body to one side, then the other. Keep hips still. (can be done seated)
  • Side Bend: Standing or seated, bend your body to one side reaching down, then the other. (can be done seated)
  • Back Extension: While seated, gently lean back, arching your back slightly. Return to upright. (can be done seated)
  • Shoulder Blade Squeeze: Squeeze your shoulder blades together behind you. Hold, then release. (can be done seated)

Stop if it hurts or you feel dizzy. Ask your doctor or therapist which exercises are right for you.

Speak

Each kind of speaking practice has 100 levels. Levels 1 to 3 are in the free plan.

  • Say the word: Read a word out loud.
  • Name the picture: Look at the picture and say its name.
  • Build a sentence: Put the words in order, then say it.
  • Finish the sentence: Pick the word that fits the sentence.
  • Listening: Listen, then pick the right picture.
  • Word match: Remember the cards and find the pairs.

Think

Short games in 3 groups. The easiest level of every game is in the free plan.

Memory

  • Remember the order: Watch the squares light up, then tap them in order.
  • Find the pairs: Turn over cards and find the matching ones.

Attention

  • Dim sum pick: Find the dim sum Bonet asks for.
  • Fruit pick: Pick the right fruit before time runs out.

Thinking

  • Tap in order: Tap numbers from small to big, then numbers and letters.
  • Math pick: Work out the sum and pick the right answer.
