Camera, body position and hand position policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Coglix Labs, Inc. makes Bonet. This page explains, in plain words, what Bonet's camera does with the picture of you. It is also our written policy for the purposes of laws about biometric information, including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act.

What does the camera do?

For exercises that count your movements, and for brain games you can play by holding your hand over an answer, Bonet uses your device's camera. A model that runs on your own device finds the position of points on your body and hands in each picture (for example, where your wrist and shoulder are) so that Bonet can count repetitions and see that you are moving.

What do we not do?

  • We do not save the picture, the video, the points, or any body or hand shape. They exist only in your device's memory for a moment and are discarded straight away.
  • We do not send them to our servers or to anyone else. The only things Bonet saves are numbers about your practice, such as how many repetitions you did, how long you held a position, a range-of-motion estimate, and how well the camera could follow the movement.
  • We do not use them to identify you or anyone else, and we do not compare them to any other picture or person.
  • We do not use face recognition or face measurements.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade or share them, and we do not use them for advertising or to train AI models.

How long do we keep it?

We keep none of it. Camera pictures and the body and hand points are destroyed immediately after they are used, and in any case as soon as you leave the exercise, close the app or turn the camera off. There is nothing for us to keep, so there is nothing to delete later. The practice numbers described above are kept in your account until you delete it (see the privacy policy).

What is my choice?

The camera is used only after you tap "I agree, start the camera" the first time, and again if this policy changes in a way that matters. If you choose "No camera", Bonet counts your exercises by hand (you tap +1) and the games work by tapping the screen. You can also turn the camera permission off at any time in your device's settings. You can use Bonet this way without losing access to anything.

Security and who can see it

Because nothing is stored or sent, there is nothing for anyone to take from us. Your device protects the camera with its own permission system. Nobody can see your camera: not us, and not the people who run our servers.

Changes and questions

If we ever change what the camera data is used for, we will update this page and ask for your agreement again before we use the camera. Questions: infos@coglixlabs.com. Coglix Labs, Inc., 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, DE 19713, USA.
