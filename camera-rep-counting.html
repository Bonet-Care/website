Exercise repetitions counted by your camera, at home

Bonet uses the camera on your phone, tablet or computer to count exercise repetitions while you practise at home. A model on your device finds body and hand positions, and simple rules count each repetition and time the set. Video is never recorded or uploaded. The count is an estimate, and Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

How does camera rep counting work?

  1. Put your device where the camera can see you, in a clear space with good light.
  2. A 3D guide shows the exercise.
  3. As you move, a model on your device finds body and hand positions in each picture.
  4. Simple rules count each repetition and time the set.
  5. A repetition is counted when you reach the position, hold it for about one second, and then come back to the start. The next repetition begins after you have returned.
  6. After the set you get a short tip, for example about height, steadiness, or left and right.

What does it measure?

Repetitions, how long each set takes, and estimates of how far you moved. These go into your practice history and your one-page report. See what Bonet tracks for every number it records.

Is my video private?

Camera video and images are read on your device. They are never recorded or uploaded. Bonet keeps the count, the time and the estimates, not the video.

How accurate is the count?

  • The count is an estimate. Light, camera angle and loose clothing can change it.
  • It is not a clinical measurement.
  • Do not rely on the camera to keep you safe. It can miss a loss of balance.

What do I need?

  • A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and a modern web browser. Open app.bonet.care.
  • Enough space to move, and good light.
  • A stable chair, or something sturdy to hold if you stand.

There are 34 exercises. Several are done seated, and you can skip, shorten or repeat any of them.

Each exercise in the practice library says what the camera counts for that move and how to set up.

What does it cost?

You can start free. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises, speaking levels 1 to 3 and brain games on Easy. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free, and you can cancel any time.

Is it safe for me to start?

Ask your doctor or therapist first. Start slow and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unwell. Read what to know before you start.
