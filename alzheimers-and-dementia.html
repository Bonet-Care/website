Caring for someone with Alzheimer's or dementia? What Bonet is and is not

Bonet is not an Alzheimer's or dementia app in the medical sense. It does not test memory, and it does not diagnose, slow, delay or treat Alzheimer's disease or any other dementia. A family member or carer can set it up as a simple daily routine to do together: gentle movement exercises, speaking practice and short brain games, with a calm Simple mode. Ask the person's doctor before starting.

Can Bonet help with Alzheimer's or dementia?

No. Bonet does not diagnose, slow, delay, treat or prevent Alzheimer's disease or any other dementia, and it is not a memory test. No study has looked at Bonet in people with dementia. If you are worried about changes in memory, talk to a doctor.

What could a carer use Bonet for?

  • A short daily routine of gentle movement, speaking and brain games that you do together.
  • Simple mode: one big Start button instead of a busy screen.
  • Brain games on Easy
  • Eight languages, so practice can be in the language the person knows best.

Keep sessions short and stop if the person is tired, upset or confused. You know the person best.

How do I get someone started?

  1. Make sure the person agrees to use Bonet and has checked with their doctor or therapist.
  2. Open app.bonet.care on their phone, tablet or computer. When the app asks who will use it, say you are setting it up for someone else.
  3. Answer the setup questions together. Turn on Simple mode and large text if a calmer screen helps.
  4. Clear a safe space with good light and a stable chair, and stay near for balance exercises.

What should I watch for as a carer?

You know the person best. Stop the session if they seem unwell, in pain or dizzy, and call your local emergency number for sudden face drooping, weakness or trouble speaking. Bonet does not watch over anyone and cannot call for help.

What is shared, and with whom?

  • The report is shared only when the person prints it or shares it. Bonet does not send it to family or therapists.
  • Camera video is analysed on the device and never recorded or uploaded. Voice recordings are not kept.

What is Bonet not?

  • It is not a medical device and not a therapy. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
  • It does not replace a doctor, therapist or speech-language pathologist.
  • It has not been tested in a clinical trial, and no regulator has reviewed or cleared it.
  • Its numbers are estimates from a camera and a microphone. They can be wrong.
  • It is a different app from Bold and is not connected to it.

What does it cost?

You can start free. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises, speaking levels 1 to 3 and brain games on Easy. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free, and you can cancel any time.
