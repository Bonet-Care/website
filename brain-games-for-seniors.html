Brain games for seniors: six short games at home

Bonet has six short brain games for memory, attention and quick thinking, made with older adults in mind. You choose Easy, Medium or Hard. They sit beside movement and speaking practice in one daily plan. They are games to enjoy, not a test and not a treatment.

Start free

Which brain games are in Bonet?

There are two games for memory, two for attention and two for thinking.

  • Remember the order: Watch the squares light up, then tap them in order.
  • Find the pairs: Turn over cards and find the matching ones.
  • Dim sum pick: Find the dim sum Bonet asks for.
  • Fruit pick: Pick the right fruit before time runs out.
  • Tap in order: Tap numbers from small to big, then numbers and letters.
  • Math pick: Work out the sum and pick the right answer.

Can I choose how hard the games are?

Yes. Each game has Easy, Medium and Hard levels, and you can choose any level you like. After you have played a game a few times, Bonet may suggest a level for today, a little easier or a little harder. You can ignore the suggestion. The free plan includes Easy; Premium opens every level.

Is there a time limit?

Some games have a clock. In Find the pairs there is a time limit, and the clock stops while a wrong pair is face up, so a slow look costs no time. In Math pick, each question has a limited time. You can leave a game at any time.

Do brain games improve memory or prevent decline?

Bonet makes no such claim. The games are for enjoyment and practice, a few minutes at a time. They are not a test and do not screen for any condition, and a score shows how a game went, not how your brain is doing. If you are worried about your memory, talk to your doctor.

How do I start?

  1. Open app.bonet.care in a browser on a phone, tablet or computer.
  2. Choose a brain game and a level. Start with Easy if you are not sure.
  3. Play for a few minutes, then stop whenever you like.

The brain games sit beside 34 movement exercises and speaking practice in one daily plan. See movement, speech and brain games.

What does it cost?

You can start free. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises, speaking levels 1 to 3 and brain games on Easy. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free, and you can cancel any time.
