Bonet Cookie and Device Storage Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

  • Bonet saves a few small pieces of information on your device. Most of them are needed to keep you signed in and to remember your settings.
  • We use two kinds of optional tools: website statistics on bonet.care (Google Analytics) and product analytics in the app (PostHog).
  • On the website, in every country, Google Analytics stays off until you click Accept. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we treat it as "no" and do not load Google Analytics.
  • We do not use advertising cookies. We do not use Google signals or ad personalization. We do not sell your data.
  • In the app, analytics stays off until you agree to it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy.

What this policy covers

We say "cookies" for cookies and for similar tools that store or read information on your device, such as local storage. Bonet uses two places:

  • bonet.care: the marketing website.
  • app.bonet.care: the web app. The iPhone and iPad apps use the same code and store the same items on the device inside the app. They do not use cookies.

How the website decides. The same rule applies in every country:

  • Google Analytics does not load unless you click Accept in the cookie notice. "Reject" is next to it and the same size. You can use the whole site without choosing.
  • Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track: if your browser sends either signal, we treat it as "do not load Google Analytics" and do not show the notice. You can still turn statistics on in Cookie settings.
  • Your choice is saved on your device in bonet_consent, with the version and the time. It is not sent to us. You can change it any time in Cookie settings. If you switch Google Analytics off after it was on, we stop loading it and you can clear the cookies above in your browser.

Your browser may also keep the downloaded body-tracking and speech model files in its normal cache so that they do not download each time.

What analytics sends from the app. Which screen type opened, which kind of practice started or finished, how long (in broad bands such as 1 to 3 minutes), whether you finished, plan type, language, and a random account number. It never sends your name, email, voice, speech, camera video, scores, the words you practise, or what you type. There is no screen recording. See the Privacy Policy for more.

Practice, health and speech information is not stored in cookies. It is stored on your account and, for the offline copy above, in local storage.

Payment pages

When you subscribe on the web, you leave bonet.care and Stripe's checkout page opens at checkout.stripe.com. Stripe sets its own cookies there to process the payment and prevent fraud. We do not control them; see Stripe's own cookie information. In the App Store, Apple handles payment under its own terms.

Our choices in the app

  • Analytics is off until you agree to it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy. When you switch it off, the app stops sending events.
  • If your browser or device sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, analytics stays off.

How to block cookies yourself

All browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data. If you block the items marked "strictly necessary", you will be signed out and some features will not work. On a shared computer, sign out when you finish.

For people in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

On the website we rely on your consent for analytics, under the national laws that implement the ePrivacy Directive (for example section 25 of Germany's TDDDG and the UK's PECR). Consent is not needed for the strictly necessary items. See the Privacy Policy, section "If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland", for your rights and how to contact us.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters (for example a new tool), we change the date at the top.
