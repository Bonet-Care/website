For therapists and carers

How to see what someone practised, and what the app does not do.

Practice report

Bonet can produce a practice report for a date range you choose. It lists which days the person practised, the exercises, speaking levels and brain games they did, reps, range-of-motion estimates and scores, and simple trends. You can print it, save it as a PDF or share it. Free accounts can print the last two weeks; Premium covers longer ranges.

The report summarises home practice. It is not a diagnosis or a clinical assessment, and clinical decisions stay with the treating therapist or doctor.

Setting it up for someone else

Family, carers and therapists can set up the app for someone else when the app asks who will use it. The person should agree, practise safely, and check with their own doctor or therapist about what suits them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and it does not replace a therapist visit.
  • There are no clinic dashboards, remote monitoring, alerts or team workspaces in the app today.
  • It is not meant to hold patient records. We do not sign Business Associate Agreements.
