What you practise

Move

Arms and shoulders, hands, sit-to-stand, legs and balance.

  1. See itA 3D guide shows the move.
  2. Do itThe camera counts each rep.
  3. Get a tipHeight, steadiness, left and right. The next plan adjusts.

Speak

Say words, name pictures, build sentences, listen.

  1. Say itPick a level and say the words aloud.
  2. Get a hintStuck? Hints grow one step at a time.
  3. See what it heardWords heard, pace and pauses. Items return for review.

Think

Memory, attention and quick thinking, in six games.

  1. Play a roundShort rounds you can start any time.
  2. It follows youDifficulty adjusts to how you do.
  3. See resultsYour results over time.
