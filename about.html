About Bonet

Bonet (Chinese name 哥基健動) is a daily home practice app for older adults, including people practising at home after a stroke. It is made by Coglix Labs Inc. and runs in a web browser at app.bonet.care. It is a practice tool, not a medical device.

Start free

What does Bonet include?

  • Move: 34 exercises with a 3D guide. The camera on your device counts each repetition.
  • Speak: say words, name pictures, build sentences and listen, with hints that grow one step at a time. 100 levels per practice type.
  • Think: six short brain games for memory, attention and quick thinking.
  • A one-page practice report you can print or share with a therapist or family.
  • A corgi coach named Bonet.

Who is Bonet for?

  • Older adults who want to keep active at home.
  • People practising at home after a stroke or an injury.
  • People practising between physiotherapy or speech therapy visits.
  • Family members and carers who help them.

What is Bonet not?

  • It is not a medical device and not a therapy. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
  • It does not replace a doctor, therapist or speech-language pathologist.
  • It has not been tested in a clinical trial, and no regulator has reviewed or cleared it.
  • Its numbers are estimates from a camera and a microphone. They can be wrong.
  • It is a different app from Bold and is not connected to it.

How much does it cost?

You can start free. The free plan includes 8 of the 34 exercises, speaking levels 1 to 3 and brain games on Easy. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free, and you can cancel any time.

See pricing for what each plan includes.

Which languages is it in?

Eight: English, Cantonese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German and French.

What happens to my camera video and voice?

  • Camera video is analysed on your device. It is never recorded or uploaded.
  • Voice recordings are not kept.
  • There are no ads, and your data is not sold.

Read the privacy policy and what Bonet tracks.

Are there studies or reviews of Bonet?

Not yet. No studies of Bonet have been published, and we do not show ratings or user numbers.
