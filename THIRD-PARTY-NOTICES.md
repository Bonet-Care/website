The animated demos in `demos/` are built on parts of the Stageshow kit (Stage, Act, Phone, Window, Roll, Chart and helpers), copied into `demos/src/kit/` and restyled with Bonet's own tokens, fonts, icons and screens. The demo choreography follows its examples (phone-touch, payout-flow, choreography, chart-scrub, device-pair, chrome, bento).
