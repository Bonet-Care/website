How it went

The camera looks at how the movement went, inspired by what therapists look for: how far it went, pace, steadiness, left and right balance, hold time and how much of the set you finished. A six-sided chart shows today's shape with last time behind it.

The same idea for speaking: words heard, saying it without hints, pace, pauses, time to start and consistency across the session.

These are automatic measures of home practice and general practice tips. They are not a clinical assessment or medical advice.

What is not kept

  • Camera video and images. They are read on your device and never recorded or uploaded.
  • Voice recordings. Bonet keeps the text and the measures, not the audio.
  • Your card number, your precise location, your contacts, photos or files.

Bonet does not use your results to train models, sell them or show ads.

What the numbers are

They are estimates from a camera and a microphone. They show what was practiced. They can be wrong, and a single day says little. Bonet is a practice tool, not a medical device.

The full detail is in How Bonet measures and the Privacy Policy. See an example report.
