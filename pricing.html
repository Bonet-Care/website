Pricing

You can start Bonet free. Premium is US$9.99 a month, with 7 days free for new subscribers, and you can cancel any time. There are no ads.

Start free

What is in the free plan?

  • Speaking levels 1 to 3 of 100
  • 8 of 34 exercises
  • Brain games on Easy
  • Last 7 days of progress
  • Report for the last 2 weeks
  • Streak, league, outfits

What does Premium include?

  • All 100 speaking levels
  • All 34 exercises, your own plans
  • Every brain game level
  • Full history and report
  • Everything in Free

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get 7 days of Premium free. You can cancel any time. Renewal, cancelling and refunds are explained on the subscriptions page.

Do I need to pay to try all three kinds of practice?

No. The free plan includes some movement exercises, speaking practice and brain games, so you can try each kind before you decide.
